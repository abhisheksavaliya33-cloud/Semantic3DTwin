Process Overview

Simple overview of how the uploaded video is converted into a semantic 3D digital twin.
1
Video Input
The uploaded indoor video is read and representative frames are extracted.
✓ COMPLETE
2
Object Detection
YOLOv8n detects supported indoor objects in the extracted frames.
✓ COMPLETE
3
Depth Estimation
Depth Anything V2 estimates monocular relative depth for each processed frame.
✓ COMPLETE
4
3D Reconstruction
Open3D converts RGB and depth information into per-frame point clouds.
✓ COMPLETE
5
Point Cloud Fusion
FPFH, RANSAC and ICP register frames and merge them into one room point cloud.
✓ COMPLETE
6
Semantic Fusion
Repeated object observations are combined into a final semantic object map.
✓ COMPLETE
7
Room Intelligence
Semantic evidence is used to infer the room type and produce the final digital twin report.
✓ COMPLETE
