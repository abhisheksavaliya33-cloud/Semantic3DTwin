Image-Based Digital Twin

Upload a single indoor room image to generate object detections, relative depth, a colored point cloud, semantic 3D objects and digital twin JSON.

🏠
Select a Room Image
Supported formats: JPG, JPEG and PNG

Depth values and reconstructed coordinates are relative estimates from a monocular RGB image. They are not metric room measurements.

Video-Based Digital Twin

Upload a short indoor video. The system samples frames, detects objects, estimates depth and creates an independent point-cloud reconstruction for each processed frame.

🎬
Select an Indoor Video
Supported formats: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV and WEBM
For faster testing, use a short indoor video.

Current video processing generates independent per-frame 3D reconstructions. Global multi-frame registration and point-cloud fusion are the next stage of the project.
